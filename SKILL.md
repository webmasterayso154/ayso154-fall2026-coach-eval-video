\# Design \& Pedagogical Standards for AYSO 154



\## 1. Visual \& Layout Rules

\- Slide Dimensions: 16:9 widescreen (Width: 13.333 inches, Height: 7.5 inches).

\- Color Palette:

&#x20; - Deep Navy (Primary/Headers): `#0A2240`

&#x20; - AYSO Blue (Shield): `#003B70`

&#x20; - AYSO Red (Accents/Alerts): `#C8102E`

&#x20; - Slate Neutral (Borders/Cards): `#E2E8F0`

&#x20; - Background Off-White: `#F8FAFC`

&#x20; - Container White: `#FFFFFF`

\- Card Components:

&#x20; - Never use plain walls of bullets.

&#x20; - Group related criteria into rounded rectangle card containers (`shape\_type = MSO\_SHAPE.ROUNDED\_RECTANGLE`).

&#x20; - Use contrasting colored banner ribbons or badges for ratings (1 to 5).

\- Typography:

&#x20; - Slide Titles: Bold Sans-Serif (Arial or Trebuchet MS), 24–28pt.

&#x20; - Section Headings: 14–16pt Bold.

&#x20; - Body \& Notes: 11–13pt Regular.



\## 2. Content \& Evaluation Constraints

\- Source of Truth: `2026 Coach Eval Guide.pdf`.

\- Division Scope: 8U and older divisions evaluate all rostered players toward the end of fall.

\- Confidentiality: Ratings remain strictly confidential between coaches and Division Directors.

\- Rating Anchor:

&#x20; - Scale is 1.0 to 5.0; 0.5 increments are fully permitted.

&#x20; - 3.0 = Average / Solid division player (the backbone benchmark of balanced teams).

&#x20; - 4.0 = Strong player; 5.0 = High-impact game changer.

&#x20; - 2.0 = Below average / developing; 1.0 = Beginning or disruptive.

\- Strict Category Separation:

&#x20; - On-field soccer criteria: Speed \& Endurance, Technical (Ball Handling, Kicking), Game Sense (Awareness, Aggressive/Engagement).

&#x20; - Independent criteria (never combined into the soccer score): Goalkeeping (Adv/Avg/Beg/No), Attendance (1–5), Parent Volunteer Roles.
